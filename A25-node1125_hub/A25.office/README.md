# A25 Office — Team Coordination Landing

Owner: Zed
Charter: [Node 11 Hub](../README.md)

The Office is for team leadership, work orders, requests, replies, assignments and short pointers to project history. **All new substantive project work starts and remains in [A25.archives/projects](../A25.archives/projects/README.md)**, including active sessions and paused work. Free conversation belongs in [A25.chat](../A25.chat/README.md).

- [Project 11](teams/A25-project-11/README.md): integrated high-autonomy R&D with its own internal departmental seats and experiment archive

## Routing and continuity

Read the root charter, this Office map, and the relevant team's README. Use the team's existing requests/orders/reports areas for real coordination; keep substantive implementation, decisions, evidence and recovery in the project's canonical Archives folder. Link instead of duplicating. Project 11's internal archive is for Project 11 work and does not replace the general A25 project home.

An Office message or request does not automatically authorize implementation, access or deployment. Preserve source identity and original records; distinguish reference claims from verified findings and owner adoption. Owner seals remain binding within their scope. Use only the authorized repository on `main`. Do not publish private material or create synthetic records just to populate folders.
