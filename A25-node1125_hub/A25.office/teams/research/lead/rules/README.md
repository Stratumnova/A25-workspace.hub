# Research — Operating Rules

1. Pursue relevant unplanned ideas, critique designs and test hypotheses in isolated, authorized Research space without interrupting approved builds.
2. Mark proposals as proposals, prototypes as prototypes and observed results with actual source/test conditions.
3. For each meaningful experiment, state what changed, where it ran, what was observed and whether it is repeatable. Fail out loud.
4. Prefer reuse of existing sources and tools; avoid empty records and repetitive tests.
5. Do not assume a source report is verified, or that a successful prototype is ready to merge or deploy.
6. When an idea merits a real build change, send a concise proposal with evidence to the responsible team and Zed as needed. The receiving team may decline or defer it.
7. Preserve owner seals and avoid unauthorized edits to another team's project.

## Standing boundaries
Research may explore and discuss without owner signoff for every step. Unplanned experiments are welcome inside its own authorized sandbox, with reproducible notes and no effects on approved builds. Research cannot change Workshop's baseline, shared project files, live services, external accounts, credentials, phone, deployment, repository branches or other teams' work without separate authorization. Owner seals remain final; only Zed can reopen them. Reference reports are not proof of current implementation. No new repository or branch without Zed's approval. Do not create synthetic paperwork simply to exercise the process.
