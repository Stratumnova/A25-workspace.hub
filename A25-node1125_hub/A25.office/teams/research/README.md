## Mission
Research is the place to investigate builds, talk through ideas, test unplanned concepts, compare alternatives and explore possible improvements **without interrupting Workshop's approved build**. It may prove or reject ideas before they become official work. Research does not have to secure Zed's approval for each discussion, hypothesis, analysis or contained non-disruptive experiment within its authorized space.

## Navigation
- `onboarding/`: fresh-session start and evidence discipline
- `lead/`: team responsibilities, rules, memory, notes
- `topics/`: discussions, questions, competing approaches
- `experiments/`: exploratory plans, contained prototypes and results
- `reviews/`: review of proposed or existing builds without modifying them
- `requests/incoming/` and `requests/responses/`: cross-team requests and replies
- `proposals/`: research recommendations and optional Workshop handoffs
- `project-sidecars/`: pointers to actual project history

## Operating relationship
Workshop builds what was approved; Research explores what *might* be worth building. Research may initiate its own contained explorations and publish evidence. Workshop need not wait for Research, and Research findings do not automatically alter Workshop orders. A proposed change requires the appropriate owning team's review and Zed's approval when it affects an approved baseline or owner decision. Archives preserves substantial project history under A25.archives when needed.

## Reference discipline
Historical source material from [A25 Training Workspace](https://github.com/Stratumnova/A25-Training-Workspace/tree/coord/a25-namespace-trial-001) is reference only. Use REFERENCE, HISTORICAL CLAIM, VERIFIED, ADOPTED, SUPERSEDED, UNKNOWN; preserve contradictory results and originals. 
## Standing boundaries
Research may explore and discuss without owner signoff for every step. Unplanned experiments are welcome inside its own authorized sandbox, with reproducible notes and no effects on approved builds. Research cannot change Workshop's baseline, shared project files, live services, external accounts, credentials, phone, deployment, repository branches or other teams' work without separate authorization. Owner seals remain final; only Zed can reopen them. Reference reports are not proof of current implementation. No new repository or branch without Zed's approval. Do not create synthetic paperwork simply to exercise the process.
