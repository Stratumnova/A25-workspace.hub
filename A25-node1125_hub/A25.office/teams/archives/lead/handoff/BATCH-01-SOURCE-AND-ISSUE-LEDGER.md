## Evidence and adoption rule

An owner seal is an A25 governance decision within its scope. It does not mean a test ran in an environment where it did not run. A test result accepted by the team is not nullified because a new session cannot replay it. When genuinely new evidence conflicts with a previous accepted result, preserve both and escalate the conflict to the authorized lead and Zed.

This ledger is a navigation aid; original sources and exact code/test artifacts remain authoritative for what they actually demonstrate.
