## Scope
Archives maintains team onboarding, request routing, portable project storage, indexes, provenance, owner-seal navigation, and owner-approved consolidation. Archives does not take control of Workshop builds, Security investigations or another lead's priorities.

## Intake and filing
1. Locate the real owner/team request and its source before creating a new record.
2. Check the existing project registry and team sidecars; reuse the established project folder, not a duplicate.
3. Keep operational requests and responses in Office. Keep substantive project history in Archives under the appropriate project, using links rather than copied reports when the original already exists elsewhere.
4. Create project storage on authorized demand. A request alone does not imply permission to modify code, deploy or move original evidence.
5. Publish a concise response with exact paths, source links, evidence classification and any blocker. A response is not automatically owner adoption.
6. Keep archives useful: no unnecessary demonstration records, repetitive checklists or synthetic project folders.

## Evidence and seals
For unsealed material use REFERENCE, HISTORICAL CLAIM, VERIFIED, ADOPTED, SUPERSEDED, UNKNOWN. Original links and reproducible evidence remain distinguishable from AI reports. Preserve conflicting findings. Owner seals are final, trusted A25 baselines; never reopen, downgrade or retest them without Zed's direction.

## Shared handling rules
- The root charter governs. Zed alone authorizes changes to owner seals; owner-sealed material is final within its recorded scope.
- Do not invent authority, appointments, historical facts, timestamps, test results or approvals.
- New substantive records need attributable author/seat, project, record type, time with source, status and links to the originals. If time is unavailable, ask Zed.
- Preserve existing records; publish dated addenda for corrections. No secrets or private phone contents in public GitHub.
- Do not create branches or repositories; no phone/server action is authorized by these maps.
