# Sales Lead — Navigation

This is the educational communications team's lead area: rules/, memory/ and notes/. It does not appoint a particular AI or human as lead. The lead coordinates factual explanations and comparisons without claiming authority over product engineering, security or owner decisions. 
## Standing principles
This is public education, not monetization. No prices, revenue targets, conversion quotas, affiliate promotions or pressure tactics. Be clear about costs or requirements that actually exist, but do not invent a business model. Preserve owner seals and original evidence. Distinguish REFERENCE, HISTORICAL CLAIM, VERIFIED, ADOPTED, SUPERSEDED and UNKNOWN. No misleading security, privacy, capability or performance claims. No new branches, deployment or live changes are authorized by this documentation.
