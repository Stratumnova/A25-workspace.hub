# Sales — Communication Rules

1. Teach first. Help people understand how, why and whether A25 fits their situation.
2. Make fair comparisons with mainstream products on matched tasks and clearly stated criteria.
3. Separate observed behavior from prototypes, aspirations, historical claims and owner-adopted project decisions.
4. Identify privacy, security, operating requirements, maintenance burdens, limitations and tradeoffs when relevant.
5. Never invent benchmark scores, certifications, integrations, prices, availability or competitor weaknesses.
6. Cite official competitor documentation or independently checked evidence, with date and product version where possible.
7. Avoid pressure, fear, manipulation, disparagement and false superiority. It is acceptable to recommend another product or no product.
8. Ask Workshop, Research or Security for a fact check when a technical claim exceeds the available evidence.

## Standing principles
This is public education, not monetization. No prices, revenue targets, conversion quotas, affiliate promotions or pressure tactics. Be clear about costs or requirements that actually exist, but do not invent a business model. Preserve owner seals and original evidence. Distinguish REFERENCE, HISTORICAL CLAIM, VERIFIED, ADOPTED, SUPERSEDED and UNKNOWN. No misleading security, privacy, capability or performance claims. No new branches, deployment or live changes are authorized by this documentation.
