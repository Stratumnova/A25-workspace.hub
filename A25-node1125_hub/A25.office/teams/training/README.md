## Mission
Support **every A25 team** by turning repeatable tasks, team rules and reliable records into lightweight local bots or small local models that do not require the full heavy GPT/Claude reasoning stack for routine work. Training also documents when a small bot should stop and hand the task to a human or stronger AI.

## Navigation
- `onboarding/`: fresh-session start and scope
- `lead/`: rules, memory and continuity
- `team-bots/`: bot responsibilities by A25 team
- `local-models/`: lightweight model and non-LLM approaches
- `datasets/`: permitted source preparation, provenance and filtering
- `skills-and-workflows/`: deterministic routines, prompts and tools
- `evaluations/`: reproducible accuracy, failure and resource checks
- `handoffs/`: proposals to owning teams and deployment requests
- `requests/incoming/` and `requests/responses/`: team requests and answers
- `project-sidecars/`: links to permanent project histories

## Approach
Prefer the simplest effective implementation: rules, indexes, templates and scripts before a trained local model; small models when learned behavior is needed. Optimize for offline/local operation, low memory, low compute, clear provenance, recoverability and controlled permissions. Never require a heavyweight AI to perform a deterministic lookup if a reliable lightweight tool can do it.

Training does not appoint a team lead or automatically operate another team's bots. Workshop handles authorized integration, Security handles security reviews, Research may explore alternative methods, Archives keeps source history, and Zed controls adoption and live deployment.

## Standing boundaries
Training builds and evaluates lightweight local bots, not replacements for owner authority or unrestricted AI agents. Team source records remain reference until verified or owner-adopted. Preserve original evidence, disagreements and valid owner seals. Label experiments, capabilities and limitations honestly. Do not claim a bot is trained, deployed, accurate or safe without corresponding evidence. This folder does not authorize access to private data, downloads of model weights, training runs on the phone, installation, live service changes, new repositories or branches, or deployment to another team.
