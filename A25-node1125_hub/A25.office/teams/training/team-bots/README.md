# Team Bot Registry — Map

Each A25 team may request or sponsor small local bots. Candidate areas include Archives indexing, Workshop build-state summaries, Security evidence routing, Audits source comparisons, Legal-Private public-safe navigation only, Research topic indexing, Sales approved explanations, Training self-maintenance, and A25-project-11 project navigation. These are examples, not approved or implemented bots.

## Actual requirements captured
- [Workstation Assistant Bot](../../../../A25.archives/projects/workstation-assistant-bot/README.md): future document preparation, section-specific templates and validation before Office/Archives filing; not built or assigned.
- [Archives Bot](../../../../A25.archives/projects/archives-bot/README.md): future indexing, preservation and recovery assistant; not built or assigned.
- [Security Bot — canonical project home](../../../../A25.archives/projects/security-bot/README.md): future change-audit bot requirements sourced from Security note A25-SEC-N003. Training may contribute lightweight implementation methods **after** scope and assignment are authorized. No training run, bot or deployment exists by virtue of this link.

Each actual bot needs a team owner, precise scope, inputs, outputs, permissions, escalation and status. Keep actual project history in Archives, not a second Training project folder.

## Standing boundaries
Training builds and evaluates lightweight local bots, not replacements for owner authority or unrestricted AI agents. Team source records remain reference until verified or owner-adopted. Preserve original evidence, disagreements and valid owner seals. Label experiments, capabilities and limitations honestly. Do not claim a bot is trained, deployed, accurate or safe without corresponding evidence. This folder does not authorize access to private data, downloads of model weights, training runs on the phone, installation, live service changes, new repositories or branches, or deployment to another team.
