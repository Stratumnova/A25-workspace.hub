# A25 Project 11 — Integrated Advanced R&D

Owner: Zed
Status: Owner-authorized special experimental department
Charter: https://github.com/Stratumnova/A25-workspace.hub/blob/main/A25-node1125_hub/README.md

## Purpose
Project 11 tackles deep technical questions, difficult edge cases, unconventional designs and experimental full-stack builds. It combines the functions of A25's departments **inside one self-contained team**. It does not routinely task or depend on the operational Workshop, Security, Archives, Audits, Legal-Private, Research, Sales or Training teams. Its departmental leads are Project 11 internal roles, not appointments over the main departments.

## Layout
- `onboarding/`: recovery and starting guidance
- `lead/`: central R&D leadership, rules, memory and notes
- `departments/`: an internal lead area for each A25 department
- `projects/`: real Project 11 experimental builds
- `lab/`: exploration, prototypes and edge-case investigation
- `integration/`: combining internal departmental work
- `reviews/`: internal safety, evidence and build review
- `requests/`: Project 11 intake and internal replies
- `handoffs/`: optional outward proposals requiring explicit adoption
- `archives/`: Project 11's own project history, source evidence, decisions, recovery and build records

## Autonomy and boundaries
Project 11 can select and pursue its own contained research topics, organize internal departmental work and iterate prototypes without repeatedly seeking owner direction. It keeps its own archive and internal decisions, while retaining traceable source evidence. The same A25 rules apply; the central owner retains control over owner seals, production adoption and resources outside this team's authorized scope.

Project 11 has broad autonomy for contained research and experimental builds within resources already authorized to it. Its internal departmental leads operate inside Project 11, not as the operational departments. No implied permission to change live services, shared baselines, credentials, phone files, private records, repositories or branches. Owner seals and direct owner decisions remain binding. Preserve original sources and distinguish REFERENCE, HISTORICAL CLAIM, VERIFIED, ADOPTED, SUPERSEDED and UNKNOWN. Report failures and disagreements rather than inventing outcomes.
