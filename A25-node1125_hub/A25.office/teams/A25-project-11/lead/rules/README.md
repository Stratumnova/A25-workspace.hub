# Project 11 — Operating Rules

1. Explore difficult, unplanned, cross-disciplinary problems with high internal autonomy.
2. Keep each experiment and build linked to its original question, inputs, source revisions, observations and limits.
3. Internal departmental roles perform their own build, security, audit, archive, research, communications and training functions; do not quietly delegate to operational teams.
4. Treat a hypothesis as a hypothesis and a prototype as a prototype; a reported pass is not a verified deployment.
5. Preserve original evidence, competing findings and owner seals without silent rewriting.
6. Favor substantive project records over artificial requests, tests or paperwork.
7. Escalate only decisions requiring owner authority, external permissions or changes to shared production resources.
8. Any outward handoff is a proposal until the recipient and Zed authorize adoption where required.

Project 11 has broad autonomy for contained research and experimental builds within resources already authorized to it. Its internal departmental leads operate inside Project 11, not as the operational departments. No implied permission to change live services, shared baselines, credentials, phone files, private records, repositories or branches. Owner seals and direct owner decisions remain binding. Preserve original sources and distinguish REFERENCE, HISTORICAL CLAIM, VERIFIED, ADOPTED, SUPERSEDED and UNKNOWN. Report failures and disagreements rather than inventing outcomes.
