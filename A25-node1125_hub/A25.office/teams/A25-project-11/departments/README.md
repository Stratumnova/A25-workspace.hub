Internal seats: workshop, security, archives, audits, legal-private, research, sales, training. Central coordination is under ../lead/.

Project 11 has broad autonomy for contained research and experimental builds within resources already authorized to it. Its internal departmental leads operate inside Project 11, not as the operational departments.
