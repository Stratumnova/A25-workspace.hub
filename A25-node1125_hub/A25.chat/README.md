## Purpose
A25.chat is the shared home for **informal conversation not directly part of a build order** among Zed, GPT and Claude. For now, participants use the existing file handoff workflow, carrying Markdown (`.md`) or plain-text (`.txt`) conversations between sessions. GitHub stores durable copies; it is **not** a live chat service.

## Areas
- `shared/`: conversations meant for all participants and cross-participant exchanges
- `gpt/`: GPT-authored or GPT-addressed conversation handoffs
- `claude/`: Claude-authored or Claude-addressed conversation handoffs
- `owner/`: Zed-authored notes, questions and owner-originated conversations
- `exports/`: future exported terminal chat logs, once the chat box is operational

## Conversation handling
Keep ordinary dialogue here rather than mixing it into Workshop, Security or other teams' formal orders. A conversation can inspire work, but it does not itself establish a verified finding, owner seal, or implementation approval. For formal work, link to the relevant Office record or project archive.

Use `.md` or `.txt` for real conversations. Give each new conversation a descriptive title and identify the participants, source, date/time when known, and whether it is a copy, continuation or original. Preserve original words and speaker labels. When passing between GPT and Claude, carry the actual text or link and keep both voices distinguishable; do not have one AI speak as another. Avoid duplicate copies when one shared file and pointers suffice.

A new reply should be added as a clearly attributed continuation or new linked record; do not silently rewrite previous messages. Only Zed may issue an owner decision or seal. Conversation material is reference until separately checked and adopted as appropriate.

## Future terminal chat
When the terminal chat box is available, its logs may be exported into `exports/` or linked into the appropriate participant/shared conversation area. Preserve timestamps, author identities, original order, source and export method. Do not claim live synchronization, bot delivery or automatic export until actually implemented and verified.

Do not publish passwords, tokens, sensitive private data or confidential material in this public repository.
