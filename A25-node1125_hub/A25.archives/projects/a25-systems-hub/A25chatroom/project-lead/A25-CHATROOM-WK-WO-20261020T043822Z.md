## Assignment discipline
- Only one implementation stage active at a time unless the Lead explicitly scopes independent parallel review.
- Every builder assignment specifies input, owned paths, no-touch paths, deliverables, test requirements, and outbox report location.
- Builder must stop and report exact blockers rather than improvise around boundaries.
- Lead independently checks evidence and records accepted/rework/blocked; failures stay visible until retested.
- At each milestone, update this project-lead area with verified state, next order, and recovery instructions; avoid copying entire code or token-heavy transcripts into lead memory.
- Archive preserves source reports and ZIP custody; Workshop owns ZIP functionality, not Archives retention policy.
- Future components (terminal, tunnel, auth system, etc.) get their own sibling project only when authorized. Chatroom may implement necessary internal identity enforcement, but must not silently build an external identity service.
- Nothing moves to sibling `main build/` until independently verified and separately approved.
